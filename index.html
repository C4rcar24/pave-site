Features
Everything you need to park
Parking intelligence, sensor-powered availability, and smart rerouting in one app.

Destination-based suggestions

PAVR shows the nearest and most relevant parking options close to where the user is actually going.

Sensor-powered availability

Our sensors detect whether supported parking spaces are occupied or open and feed that data into PAVR in real time.

Automatic rerouting

As drivers approach their destination, PAVR can reroute them to the best nearby available parking option instead of letting them circle.

Legal parking guidance

PAVR helps users understand whether a space is allowed, how long they can stay, and when they may need to move.

Before-arrival parking intelligence

PAVR activates before users arrive so they can decide sooner based on distance, availability, and convenience.

Operator visibility

Parking operators and pilot partners can use PAVR to understand space activity and improve parking visibility for drivers.

Our Mission
Making parking faster, smarter, and less frustrating.
PAVR exists to help drivers stop wasting time circling for parking and start making better parking decisions before they arrive.

Why PAVR exists

Parking is one of the most frustrating parts of driving. People lose time, burn gas, miss appointments, and risk tickets simply because they do not know where parking is actually available. PAVR was built to change that.

Our mission is to make parking more visible, more efficient, and easier to understand by combining destination-based guidance, sensor-powered availability, and smart rerouting into one experience.

What we want to change

PAVR is designed to reduce circling, improve parking visibility, and help drivers reach their destination with less stress. We want to make cities easier to navigate, help operators better understand their parking activity, and create a smarter parking experience for everyone.

As PAVR expands, our goal is to bring this experience to more supported lots, garages, curb zones, campuses, and cities.
